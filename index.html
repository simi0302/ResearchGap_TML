ABOUT THE PROJECT

About ResearchGap / 關於本專案

ResearchGap cross-analyzes patents and academic literature so researchers can quickly gauge how crowded a technology area is and spot underexplored "white-space" opportunities before committing to a research direction or patent filing. ResearchGap 是一套專利與學術文獻白地分析平台，協助研發人員在投入研究或申請專利前，快速掌握技術主題的競爭與研究密度，找出尚未被充分探索的「白地」方向。

The problem 現況痛點

Finding gaps today means manually combing patent databases and journals — slow, and easy to miss things.

現行做法多仰賴人工逐篇檢索專利庫與期刊，憑經驗判斷，耗時且易遺漏。

White-space matrix 白地矩陣

Crowded, developing and gap areas become visible at a glance, computed from the real patent corpus.

以互動矩陣呈現各技術組合的專利密度，一眼看出高密度區、發展中區與潛在白地，數字均來自真實專利計算。

AI that explains, not invents 數據與判讀分離

Scores and counts come from backend computation; the AI assistant only explains them and suggests next questions.

分數與統計皆由後端程式計算，AI 只負責解釋與提出建議問題，不自行生成數字，避免幻覺風險。

Case & goal 案例與目標

Validated on SDN/NFV networking with 2,799 patents and a knowledge base of 8,000+ papers that grows with every search — aiming to make gap-finding a self-service, data-driven tool.

以 SDN／NFV 為案例，交叉比對 2,799 筆專利與八千餘筆論文（知識庫隨每次搜尋持續累積），目標是讓「找研究缺口」成為人人可自助使用的資料驅動工具。

Patent & Literature White-Space Analysis Platform · 專利文獻白地分析平台

Real patent search, White-Space Matrix (2,799 patents), a growing knowledge base & AI assistant — no placeholder data. 真實專利搜尋、白地矩陣（2,799 筆）、持續成長的知識庫與 AI 助手——沒有示意資料。
