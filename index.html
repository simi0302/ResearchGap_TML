ABOUT THE PROJECT

About ResearchGap / 關於本專案

ResearchGap cross-analyzes patents and academic literature so researchers can quickly gauge how crowded a technology area is and spot underexplored "white-space" opportunities before committing to a research direction or patent filing. ResearchGap 是一套專利與學術文獻白地分析平台，協助研發人員在投入研究或申請專利前，快速掌握技術主題目前的競爭與研究密度，找出尚未被充分探索的「白地」方向。

Traditional searches rely on manually combing databases and journals — slow and easy to miss things. A white-space table and trend charts make crowded, developing, and gap areas visible at a glance. 現行做法多仰賴人工逐篇檢索專利庫與期刊論文，憑經驗判斷哪些交叉領域仍有機會，過程耗時且易遺漏。ResearchGap 將專利與文獻數據交叉比對，以表格呈現各技術組合的研究與專利密度，讓使用者一眼看出高密度區、發展中區與潛在白地，並提供趨勢圖表追蹤成長速度。

An AI research assistant answers natural-language questions about gaps, patents, and possible research questions. Numbers always come from real backend computation — the AI only explains and suggests, never fabricates figures. 系統另搭載 AI 研究助手，可用自然語言詢問特定組合是否存在白地、相關專利或研究問題。平台採「數據與判讀分離」設計：白地分數與統計數字皆由後端程式計算得出，AI 僅負責解釋與提出建議問題，不自行生成或臆測數量指標，避免生成式 AI 的幻覺風險。

The prototype validates this approach on SDN/NFV networking, cross-analyzing over two thousand patents and papers, with a bilingual, fully responsive interface. 原型以 SDN／NFV 網路技術為案例，交叉比對逾兩千筆專利與文獻，驗證白地矩陣在真實資料上的可用性，介面採中英雙語，並完成響應式排版。

The goal: turn finding research gaps from a task that depends on senior researchers' experience into a self-service, data-driven tool for anyone — speeding up technology selection and patent strategy. ResearchGap 的目標，是把「找研究缺口」從仰賴資深研究者經驗與大量人工檢索，轉變為任何研究者都能自助使用的資料驅動工具，加速技術選題與專利佈局決策效率。
