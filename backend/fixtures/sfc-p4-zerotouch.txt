Zero-Touch Service Function Chaining over Kubernetes-Based NFV Infrastructure

Published 2022. (c) 2022 ACM.

We propose an intent-based SDN controller for SFC provisioning across Kubernetes-based
NFV infrastructure, using P4 programmable switches for flow table offload and zero-touch
closed-loop automation. The controller automatically re-provisions a service chain when a
programmable switch reports a fault, without operator intervention, using a declarative
configuration model for the desired chain topology.
