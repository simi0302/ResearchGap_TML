Federated Learning-Based Intelligent Controller for O-RAN Radio Resource Management

Published 2023. (c) 2023 IEEE.

This paper proposes a federated learning based O-RAN intelligent controller (RIC) for
intent-based radio resource management, integrating xApps for beamforming optimization
across gNB and enb nodes in a cloud-native RAN deployment. The proposed architecture
allows distributed massive MIMO scheduling decisions to be trained locally at each site
without sharing raw channel state information, improving spectral efficiency while
preserving operator data privacy.
