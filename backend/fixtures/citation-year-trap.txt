Section 1. Introduction

Prior work (Smith et al., 2022) and (Jones, 2021) discussed similar ideas [Lee et al., 2022].
We build on that 2022 baseline extensively, discussing the 2022 dataset repeatedly
throughout this section 2022 2022 2022. This paper proposes an intent-based SDN controller
for adaptive network slicing that responds to real-time traffic prediction using
reinforcement learning.
